My Tribute to Robert DeNiro


Robert Anthony De Niro Italian born August 17th, 1943 is an American actor and producer.
Widely regarded as one of the Greatest and most influential actors of his generation.
He recieve Various Accolades including two Academy Awards and a Golden Awards. Many of De Niro films are considered classics of America cinemas.
Several journalists consider De Niro to be among the BEST actors of his generation.


Robert De Niro is known for dramatic roles in:


  • Bernie Madoff (2006)
  • Analyze This(1999)
  • Ronin (1998)
  • Goodfellas(1990)

De Niro attended PS 41, a public elementary school in Manhattan, through the sixth grade. He began acting classes at the Dramatic Workshop and made his stage debut in school at age 10, playing the Cowardly Lion in The Wizard of Oz. He later went to Elisabeth Irwin High School, the upper school of the Little Red School House, for the seventh and eighth grades. He was then accepted into the High School of Music & Art for the ninth grade, but attended for only a short time before transferring to a public junior high school: IS 71, Charles Evans Hughes Junior High School. De Niro attended high school at McBurney School and later, Rhodes Preparatory School.
